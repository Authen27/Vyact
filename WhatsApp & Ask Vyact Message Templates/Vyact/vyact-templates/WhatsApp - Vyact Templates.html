How to read this board
Every template: one body, its variables, and its variants.
Header images are the shipped assets in media/ — 1200×628, generic by necessity because Meta locks header media at approval. All data lives in the body.
The variant rule — read this first
same templateOnly the variable values differ. One Meta submission covers both.
separate templateThe sentence structure differs, so it is a different approved template and needs its own submission. You cannot swap a body at send time.

Copy rules
  • Multi-line. One fact per line so it survives a notification preview.
  • Greeting only where the news is neutral or good.
  • One emoji at most, never on money slipping away.
  • Every Result names its undo. Every Nudge names its off switch.
  • Every Forecast names the assumption it rests on.

Interpret & Forecast · session turns. The PIN challenge and the routed refusal are what make the channel's rules legible rather than arbitrary: pip answers spend, budget and bill questions freely, gates the three figures that describe wealth, and always names where a thing can be done. large_transaction_alert is the one Nudge with no off switch, because a fraud alert isn't a preference.
